Privacy Policy & Data Safety

SVCET CampusWash • Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering & Technology
Last Updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SVCET CampusWash ("we", "our", or "the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how our smart hostel laundry application collects, uses, and safeguards information provided by students and college staff of Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering and Technology (SVCET).

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to schedule, process, track, and return hostel laundry bags:

  • Identity & Hostel Information: Full Name, Student Roll Number / ID, Academic Year (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Year), Hostel Block name, and Room Number.
  • Contact Details: Mobile Phone Number used strictly for sending real-time SMS / in-app ready-for-pickup notifications.
  • Clothes Intake Verification Photos: Images of clothing items captured or selected by students to verify piece counts during counter drop-off.

🔒 Photo Data Safety: Uploaded photos are automatically compressed on-device into lightweight thumbnails (<30 KB). They are never analyzed for biometric data and are never shared with external third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To assign designated weekly drop-off and pickup dates according to academic year schedules.
  • To generate unique numeric Pickup Tokens (e.g., #LND-XXXX) for secure counter clothes collection.
  • To allow hostel staff to cross-verify physical clothes bags with student digital checklists.
  • To notify students when laundry transitions to Ready for Pickup.

4. Data Storage & Encryption

All communications between the mobile application and our cloud servers are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption. Personal records are stored in secure, access-controlled MySQL databases.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Advertising

Zero Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade student personal information to advertisers, tracking networks, or commercial third parties. The app contains no advertisements.

6. Permissions Requested

  • CAMERA: Used optionally when the user chooses to snap photos of their clothes bag directly inside the app.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used optionally to select photos from the device gallery.

7. User Rights & Account / Data Deletion

In strict adherence to Google Play Developer policies, any student or staff member may request the complete, permanent deletion of their account and all associated booking history and photos:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the App → Navigate to Profile → Tap "Request Account & Data Deletion".
  • Administrative Request: Contact the campus hostel administration or email laundry-support@svcet.edu.in with your registered phone number.
