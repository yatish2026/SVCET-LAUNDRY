Account & Data Deletion

VASTRA — Smart Hostel Laundry Management
RVS University Campus

1. Overview

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies on User Data & Account Deletion, students and staff who use VASTRA have the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

2. Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon submitting an account deletion request, the following information will be permanently purged:

  • User Profile: Full Name, Student Roll Number / Staff ID, Academic Year, Room Number, Hostel Block, and Phone Number.
  • Authentication Data: User Login Credentials and Session Tokens.
  • Laundry Records: All past and active laundry booking submissions and token histories.
  • Verification Media: All clothing verification intake photos stored on the server.
  • Support Tickets: Any logged complaint or technical assistance records.
⏱️ Deletion Timeline: Your account and all corresponding data records will be permanently deleted from our production servers within 48 hours of verification.

3. How to Request Account Deletion

You can delete your account using either of the two official methods:

Option A: Direct In-App Deletion (Instant)

  • Open the VASTRA app on your device.
  • Tap on your Profile tab in the navigation dock.
  • Scroll to the bottom and select "Request Account & Data Deletion".
  • Confirm the prompt to immediately schedule your account for permanent removal.

Option B: Email Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, you can submit a deletion request by email:

Send your deletion request with your Registered Phone Number or Student ID:

Recipient: laundry@rvsu.ac.in (or yatish2026@gmail.com)

4. Data Retention Policy

We do not retain any backup copies of user data once a deletion request has been fulfilled, except where strictly required by campus administrative audit regulations.
